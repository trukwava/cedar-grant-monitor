# Shadow-mode client pilot plan

**Objective:** Test whether a grants lead can reproduce an obligation total and catch actionable reporting problems faster than with current spreadsheet-based reconciliation. The fictional Cedar Pathways demo is not evidence of demand, accuracy, regulatory acceptance or time savings.

## Five discovery questions

1. For your next reporting window, what exact deliverables, counting units, exclusions and amendment rules are stipulated by each agreement?
2. Which authoritative export fields and identifiers would prove each reported total, and who owns data-quality corrections?
3. Which errors cost the most reconciliation time today: duplicates, late outcomes, stale exports, missing evidence, changed definitions or competing reporting calendars?
4. What alert threshold and ownership workflow would make a warning actionable rather than noisy?
5. Who reviews and approves a funder-ready submission, and what historical evidence must be reproducible six months later?

## Measurement and trial

- **Baseline:** time one real prior-period reconciliation end to end with the existing workflow. Log time to first defensible total, number of manual corrections, ambiguous source fields and missing evidence items.
- **Shadow mode:** for one completed reporting period, compare this monitor against the existing approved report. Do not let the monitor submit or make participant decisions. Review discrepancies with the grants lead.
- **Measures:** reconciled totals by metric, time to evidence trace, false-positive alerts, missed actionable issues, share of quarantined rows resolved, and reviewer confidence based on inspected evidence—not a fabricated numeric score.
- **Stop conditions:** an unexplained discrepancy in published totals, loss of source provenance, accidental cross-organization access, materially misleading freshness status, or unapproved handling of sensitive source records.
- **Next scope decision:** only after repeatable reconciliation and useful alerts, select **one** reviewed read-only CRM adapter or report-export enhancement. Defer broad predictions, external submissions and participant intake.

Snapshot verification is a pilot stop condition: a failed pinned recomputation must block report-draft generation until reviewed, not silently alter totals. Measure verifier failures and diagnosis time during the shadow trial; there are no empirical reliability claims yet.
